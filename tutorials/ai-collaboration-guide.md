# Collaborating with AI at InkuA

At InkuA, we believe in the power of human-AI collaboration. This guide outlines how to effectively work with AI tools to enhance productivity and creativity.

## The Importance of Data
Feeding data to AI and validating its output is a critical job. The quality of the AI's assistance depends directly on the quality of the information provided to it.

*   **Create Data:** Document your ideas, meetings, and decisions clearly.
*   **Feed Data:** Provide relevant context when asking AI for help.
*   **Validate:** AI is a tool, not a replacement for human judgment. Always review and verify the output.

## Best Practices

### 1. Context is Key
When working with AI, provide as much context as possible.
*   **Who:** Who is the audience?
*   **What:** What is the goal?
*   **Why:** Why are we doing this?

### 2. Clear Documentation
Write documentation that is easy for both humans and machines to understand.
*   **Use Markdown:** Structured text (headers, lists) helps AI parse information.
*   **Be Explicit:** Avoid ambiguity.
*   **English First:** Use English for all core documentation to ensure consistency and better AI processing.

### 3. Iterative Process
Collaborating with AI is iterative.
1.  **Draft:** Ask AI for an initial draft or idea.
2.  **Refine:** Provide feedback and ask for improvements.
3.  **Finalize:** Edit the final result yourself to ensure it aligns with InkuA's voice and values.

## Validating Work
Never blindly accept AI output. Check for:
*   **Accuracy:** Are the facts correct?
*   **Tone:** Does it sound like us?
*   **Relevance:** Does it actually solve the problem?

By treating AI as a collaborator rather than a magic wand, we can achieve more with less.
