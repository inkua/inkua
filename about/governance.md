# Governance & Decision Making

InkuA operates on a decentralized model where decision-making authority is distributed among those who contribute.

## Core Principles

1.  **Do-ocracy**: Decisions are made by those who do the work. If you see a problem, fix it. If you have an idea, propose it.
2.  **Transparency**: All decisions, plans, and outcomes should be documented and accessible to the community.
3.  **Consensus**: We strive for consensus, but action is prioritized over endless debate.

## Roles

*   **Members**: Everyone who contributes to a project.
*   **Leads/Architects**: Those who take responsibility for a project's success and guide its direction. See [Leads](../inkua/members/leads/).
*   **Advisors**: [Honorary Members](../inkua/members/honorary/) who provide guidance and expertise.

## Planning & Strategy

Projects manage their own planning and strategy within their respective [Planning](../projects/active/) folders.
Global strategy is discussed in the [InkuA Core](../projects/active/inkua-core/) project (if applicable).
