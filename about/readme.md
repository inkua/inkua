# About InkuA

InkuA is a decentralized volunteer platform operating on a 'Wikipedia Model' with zero budget, focused on human collaboration.

## Who We Are
We are a community of students, professionals, and dreamers working together to build a better future through sustainable development projects.

## Our Core Documents

*   [**Manifesto**](manifesto.md): Our Mission, Vision, and Methodology (The Wikipedia Model).
*   [**History**](history.md): The story of InkuA, from 0 to 3000 members.
*   [**Governance**](governance.md): How we make decisions and organize ourselves.
