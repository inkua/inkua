# InkuA History

InkuA is not a traditional NGO. It is a massive proof of concept that human collaboration needs no budget to change the world.

### Our Story

We didn't start with a grant or a large office. We started with one person, a laptop, and a vision.

*   **The Explosion**: In just 6 months, we grew organically from 1 founder to 500 active leaders.
*   **The Scale**: Eventually, over 3,000 volunteers applied and joined our ranks, all driven by the desire to help and learn.

### What We Achieved

With nothing but talent and willpower, our volunteers executed projects that large organizations would struggle to deal with:

*   **🌍 Humanitarian Aid**: We organized complex logistics for support missions to Ukraine during the crisis. With 3 InkuA volunteers on the ground, and more than  17 helping remotely. We also presented our research in a political event in Berlin and in Lithuania. 
*   **🏘️ App Development**: We developed and deployed a mobile App used in over 4,000 favelas to ensure food distribution was fair, transparent, and reached the families who needed it most. 
*   **🕵️‍♂️ Justice & Cybercrime**: Our tech teams collaborated with cybercrime police units to dismantle two major money-laundering networks (moving over €300,000/month) and rescued +20 victims of sophisticated online scams.
*   **⚖️ Fighting Corruption**: Our PR team did an investigation on a public program.  We took on institutional corruption, conducting investigations that led to public accountability interviews with government ministers. 
*   **📚 Academic & Social Impact**: We published over 40 research papers, built websites for rural schools, and developed apps to combat gender violence.
